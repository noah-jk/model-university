Cascadia State University is a public university with two campuses in the South Puget Sound region of Washington: the Tacoma Bay campus on Commencement Bay and the Olympia campus near the state capitol, along with a growing set of fully online programs.

We were founded to give working people in the region access to a four-year education close to home, and that's still who we serve. Our students are older on average than at most universities, many work while they study, and many are the first in their families to go to college. Small classes and faculty who know students by name are the norm, not a perk.

## Teaching and research

Faculty here are teachers first, and most also do research, much of it about the region: the Salish Sea, earthquake risk, rural health care, wildfire smoke, housing, and the region's schools. Students take part early, through course-based research, paid field positions, and capstone projects with public agencies, tribes, nonprofits, and businesses.
