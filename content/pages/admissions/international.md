---

Students from more than 40 countries study at Cascadia State. If you'll need an F-1 student visa, apply as an international student, for a bachelor's degree or a graduate program.

**What's different for you:**

- **English proficiency.** Send TOEFL, IELTS, or Duolingo English Test scores unless English is your first language or you studied in English.
- **Financial documents and your I-20.** Before we can issue the I-20 you need for a visa, you show that you can cover a year of tuition and living costs.
- **Time for your visa.** Visa interviews can take weeks to book, so plan to apply early. International Student Services helps from your offer to your arrival.

Federal financial aid (the FAFSA) isn't open to international students, but merit scholarships and graduate assistantships are.
