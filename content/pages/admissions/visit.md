The best way to know whether Cascadia State is right for you is to come see it. Tours are led by current students, who are happy to answer any question, including the ones you might not ask an admissions counselor.

## Our campuses

**Tacoma Bay campus** overlooks Commencement Bay, a short walk from downtown Tacoma and the light rail. It's home to most undergraduate programs, residence halls, and the Rainier Student Center.

**Olympia campus** sits in a quiet wooded setting near the state capitol. It hosts programs in the arts, education, and public policy, along with the recital hall in Salish Hall.

## Ways to visit

- **Campus tours** run on the first Friday of each month in Tacoma and the third Friday in Olympia, October through April. Tours take about 90 minutes and go rain or shine.
- **Open houses** in fall and spring include sample classes, faculty conversations, and residence hall tours.
- **Online information sessions** for graduate programs and transfer students run throughout the year.

## Getting here

Both campuses have visitor parking; ask for a free visitor permit at the admissions desk. The Tacoma Bay campus is served by the Link light rail and several bus routes. If you need accessibility accommodations for a visit, let us know when you register and we'll plan around them.
