Graduate admission at Cascadia State happens program by program. Each program sets its own requirements and deadlines, and its faculty read your application. The Graduate School Office runs the process and can answer questions at any point.

**What's different for you:**

- **Your program's deadline is the one that counts.** Each program page lists its deadlines by start term.
- **Recommendations and transcripts.** Most programs ask for two letters of recommendation and transcripts from every college you've attended.
- **Funding.** Teaching and research assistantships cover tuition and pay a stipend. Ask your program about openings when you apply.

Applying from outside the United States? See the steps for [international students](/admissions/international/).
