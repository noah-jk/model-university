Tuition at Cascadia State is charged per credit and varies by program. Each program page lists its per-credit rate and an estimated total, and the ranges on this page show what students typically pay at each level.

## Financial aid

Most Cascadia State students receive financial aid. To be considered for grants, loans, and work-study, file the FAFSA, or the WASFA if you aren't eligible for federal aid, as early as you can after it opens. Use Cascadia State's school code so your results reach us.

- **Grants** don't need to be repaid. Washington residents from low- and middle-income families may qualify for the Washington College Grant, which can cover full tuition.
- **Scholarships** are awarded for academic achievement, field of study, and community involvement. One application puts you in the running for most university scholarships. The priority deadline is **February 1**.
- **Work-study** provides part-time campus jobs, often related to your field.
- **Loans** are available to students and parents, and financial aid counselors can help you borrow only what you need.

Financial aid counselors host FAFSA and WASFA completion nights throughout the year and can meet one-on-one in person or online.

## Paying your bill

Student Accounts offers payment plans that spread each term's bill over three or four installments with no interest.
