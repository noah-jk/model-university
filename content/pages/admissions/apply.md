## First-year students

1. **Choose a start term.** Most bachelor's programs start in fall, winter, or spring.
2. **Apply online.** The application is free for Washington residents and takes about 30 minutes.
3. **Send your transcripts.** Unofficial transcripts are fine for review; we'll ask for official copies if you're admitted.
4. **Submit a short personal statement.** Tell us about something you care about and how you've pursued it. Test scores are optional.

Fall applications are due **March 1**, winter applications **October 15**, and spring applications **January 15**. We review applications as they arrive, so applying early means hearing back early.

## Transfer students

If you've completed college credit after high school, apply as a transfer student. The Transfer Center evaluates your credits before you enroll, and students who complete a Washington direct transfer associate degree enter with junior standing in most majors. Not every program accepts transfer credit; check the program page.

## Graduate students

Graduate programs set their own requirements, which usually include a bachelor's degree, a minimum GPA, a statement of purpose, and recommendation letters or a resume. Requirements and deadlines are listed on each program page. The Graduate School Office can help with questions about assistantships and funding.

## After you apply

You'll get an admission decision by email, usually within four weeks of a complete application. Admitted students are invited to Admitted Student Day in April and to register for orientation.
