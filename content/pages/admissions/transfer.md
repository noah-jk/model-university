title: Transfer students
description: Steps, dates, and help for students transferring to Cascadia State from another college, or coming back to finish a degree.
---

Apply as a transfer student if you've earned college credit after high school, at a community college, another university, or years ago. More than half of Cascadia State's undergraduates started somewhere else.

**What's different for you:**

- **Your credits come with you.** The Transfer Center evaluates every course you've taken, and a Washington direct transfer associate degree brings junior standing in most majors. Not every program accepts transfer credit, so check the program page.
- **You send transcripts from every college,** even if you didn't finish a course there.
- **Orientation is shorter,** and focuses on how your credits apply and registering for your first classes.

Coming back after time away? The same steps apply, and an advisor can help you plan around work and family.
