---
title: Admissions
description: How to apply to Cascadia State, what it costs, and how to visit, for first-year, transfer, and graduate students.
---

Cascadia State admits students who are ready to do serious work and want to do it in the Pacific Northwest. Most of our students live and work in the region. Many are transferring from a community college, working while they study, or the first in their family to go to college.

There's no single path in. Start with the program you're interested in: each program page lists its admission requirements, start terms, and deadlines.
