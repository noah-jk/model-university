A team of senior data science students spent their capstone year working with the city's transit planners, and some of their recommendations are now part of a proposal for next year's service changes.

The students combined three years of anonymized ridership data with census and employment data to find where buses were crowded, where they ran nearly empty, and where riders faced long waits for transfers. They found that riders on two routes serving hospitals and warehouses often waited more than 30 minutes at night.

"Clean data is a myth," said Associate Professor Ana Quintero, who advised the team. "Half the project was figuring out what the data could and couldn't tell us, and being honest about that with the city."

Professor Hana Duarte, who chairs the department, said every senior in the program completes a capstone with a public agency, nonprofit, or business partner.
