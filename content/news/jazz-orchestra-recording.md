The Cascadia State Jazz Orchestra has released its first studio album, recorded over three days last spring in the university's renovated recording studio.

The album mixes standards with original work, including three compositions by current music majors. Students also handled much of the recording and mixing as part of an audio production course.

"Playing on a record is different from playing a concert," said Professor Tuan Olsen, who directs the ensemble. "You hear every detail, so the students pushed each other in a way I don't always see in rehearsals."

The album is free to stream. The Jazz Orchestra's next live performance is Winter Jazz Night on February 26 in the Salish Hall recital hall.
