Prospective students increasingly start their college search by asking an AI assistant. Starting this fall, those assistants can get their answers straight from Cascadia State.

The university now publishes its full academic catalog through the Model Context Protocol (MCP), an open standard that lets AI assistants look up information from trusted sources. Once a student connects the catalog, an assistant can search 142 programs, compare tuition and start dates, look up any of 1,330 courses, and check which student services are open right now, with a link to the official page for every answer.

"Families were already asking chatbots about us, and sometimes getting outdated answers," said Esther Kowalski, director of digital strategy. "This way the answer comes from the source, and it links back to the page where they can confirm it."

The connection is read-only and doesn't require an account. Every program, course, and service page also includes an "Ask AI" menu that opens a conversation about that page, and a plain-text copy of each page is available for assistants that read the web.

Instructions for connecting Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, and VS Code are on the [Connect your AI](/connect/) page. Admissions counselors remain available for anything an assistant can't answer.
