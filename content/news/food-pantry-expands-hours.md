The Basic Needs Center and Food Pantry is expanding its hours this fall, with afternoon openings Monday through Thursday and a new evening pickup option for students who order ahead.

Any current student can use the pantry. There is no income requirement and no paperwork, just a student ID. Fresh produce arrives weekly from regional farms, and the pantry stocks halal, vegetarian, and gluten-free options.

The center also helps students apply for state food benefits, find emergency housing, and request emergency grants, which can cover a rent gap or a car repair that would otherwise keep someone out of class.

"Students tell us the hardest part is asking," said a pantry coordinator. "We've tried to make it as easy as picking up a coffee."

The center's next Fresh Food Market, with free produce on the plaza outside Cedar Commons, is October 28.
