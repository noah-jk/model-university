Students in the Department of Geology spent the summer installing a network of small seismometers on both the Tacoma Bay and Olympia campuses. The sensors now stream data to a regional network that tracks earthquakes across the Pacific Northwest.

Each sensor was assembled and tested by students in an upper-division instrumentation course taught by Assistant Professor Adebayo Reyes. The data supports research on how different soils amplify shaking, which matters for building codes and emergency planning.

"The Cascadia Subduction Zone isn't an abstract topic here," said Professor Vikram Silva, who chairs the department. "Our students are collecting data that helps their own communities prepare."

The network also feeds a public display in the lobby of Alder Hall, where visitors can watch the ground move in real time. Silva will speak about earthquake preparedness at a free lecture on November 18.
